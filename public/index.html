askBack

askBack answers WhatsApp in private chats and in groups you turn on. It reads the chat, your files, and OpenAI. If it does not understand, it stays quiet.

Knowledge

Name each file (Meeting notes, Programme) so the bot can share the right one in WhatsApp. PDF or audio.

    Send to a group

    Type a message and askBack will post it in the WhatsApp group you pick.

    Admins

    If someone tags askBack and it has no answer, it tags these people instead of guessing.

      Groups

      askBack only answers in groups you turn on, and only in the group that asked. Replies quote the question and tag the person who asked, plus anyone they replied to or mentioned. Community hubs stay silent so a reply is not copied into every linked group.
